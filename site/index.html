The problem

Chrome prints exactly what you see - clutter included.

Purely recognizes the same patterns designers and developers actually use to build these elements, and removes them before you print. Toggle off anything you'd rather keep.

Ads

Banner, sidebar and native ad units - the single biggest source of wasted print space.

.adsbygoogle

Navigation & menus

Header bars, breadcrumbs and menus that repeat on every single page.

nav, .navbar

Pop-ups & modals

Newsletter signups, "before you go" dialogs, and paywalled overlays.

[role="dialog"]

Cookie banners

Consent strips that dock permanently at the top or bottom of the page.

#onetrust-banner-sdk

Comments & related posts

Reader discussions and "you may also like" rails at the bottom of articles.

#disqus_thread

Social & sticky bars

Share buttons and floating bars that follow you down the page.

.share-buttons
How it works

Three steps, no dialogs to fight with.

1

Click, or press a shortcut

Open the Purely popup and turn on "Clean this page" - or skip the click entirely.

Ctrl+Shift+K
2

Pick a mode

"Declutter only" keeps the original layout. "Reader mode" rebuilds a clean article view using a local content-scoring engine - no AI, no cloud, nothing leaves your browser.

3

Export a branded PDF

Chrome's own print dialog opens, with your logo and header already in place. Choose "Save as PDF" and you're done.

Ctrl+Shift+P
Under the hood

Every setting is a switch you control.

Nothing is hidden or automatic by default beyond what you choose - Purely's own settings page looks a lot like this list.

11 cleaning categories

Ads, navigation, pop-ups, cookie banners, social, comments, related posts, sticky elements, footer, background media - each on its own switch.

Smart Reader mode

A local scoring engine rebuilds a clean reading view - title, byline and date included when the page exposes them.

Custom CSS selectors

"Always remove" and "always keep" rules for the one stubborn site the defaults don't fully cover.

Auto-clean by domain

Pick sites that get cleaned automatically on every visit - opt-in, one permission click.

PDF branding

Your logo, header and footer text - visible only in the printed/PDF version, never while browsing.

English & Hebrew, full RTL

Every page, menu and notification translates instantly - the whole interface flips direction with it.

Pick element to remove

Click the popup button, then click anything automatic detection missed to hide it instantly - remembered per site so it's re-applied automatically next time.

Reader-mode typography controls

Adjust text size and line spacing live in Reader mode, remembered for next time.

Privacy by construction

There is no server to trust, because there is no server.

  • Zero network callsCleaning, reading, and branding all run on-device. Purely has no backend to connect to.
  • Your logo stays on your machineUploaded as local storage only, never uploaded anywhere - not even to sync.
  • Minimal permissions, opt-in for the restBroad site access is only requested if you turn on auto-clean for a specific domain.
  • Reversible by designCleaning hides elements, it never deletes them - restore the original page in one click.

Getting started

Installed in under a minute.

Chrome extensions install through the browser's own Developer mode - there's no separate installer to download or trust.

  1. Unzip the downloadExtract Purely-v1.5.0.zip to any folder.
  2. Open chrome://extensionsType it straight into the address bar.
  3. Turn on Developer modeTop-right toggle on the extensions page.
  4. Click "Load unpacked"Select the unzipped folder (it contains manifest.json).
