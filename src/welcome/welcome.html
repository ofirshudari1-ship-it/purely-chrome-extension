Welcome to Purely

Purely strips ads, navigation and pop-ups from any web page, then gets it ready for print or a clean PDF - with your own logo.

1

Clean with one click

Click the Purely icon in your toolbar (you may need to pin it 📌) and turn on "Clean this page".

2

Pick a mode

"Declutter only" keeps the original layout. "Reader mode" rebuilds a fully clean reading view - like Reader View.

3

Export a branded PDF

Click "Export clean PDF" - the print dialog opens with your logo and header. Choose "Save as PDF" and you're done.

💡 Tip: right-click any page and choose Purely from the menu, or use the keyboard shortcuts Ctrl+Shift+K to clean quickly and Ctrl+Shift+P to clean + PDF.

🔒 Purely runs entirely locally in your browser - no external servers, no data collection.
