## Full description (English)

Purely cleans up any web page before you print it or save it as a PDF — no more ads, sticky
navigation bars, cookie banners, newsletter pop-ups or social-share clutter in your printout.

- **Declutter only** — keeps the page's original layout, just removes the noise.
- **Reader mode** — rebuilds a clean, article-style reading view (like your browser's built-in
  reader, but tunable), scored entirely on your device.
- **Custom PDF branding** — add your own logo, header and footer text to every export.
- **Per-site memory** — Purely remembers which mode you prefer for each site.
- **Auto-clean** — pick specific domains to clean automatically, every time, no click needed.
- **100% local.** No external servers, no analytics, no tracking. Your settings never leave
  your computer.
- Full English/Hebrew UI with instant switching, light/dark theme, and system-theme detection.
